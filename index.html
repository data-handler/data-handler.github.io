Gelder Group · Timesheets

From the van to the payroll run

Before

Handwritten sheets, spreadsheets and phone calls. Late submissions, no way of knowing whether anyone was where they said they were, and a weekly argument about hours worked versus hours paid. All of it typed in by hand.

Now

Around 120 tradespeople submit from a simple app on their phone. Every sheet is checked against vehicle tracker data and matched to real jobs before anyone in the office sees it. Nobody re-keys the hours.

Behind the app, an integration service pulls each submission, enriches it with TN360 and Quartix tracker data, and matches it to jobs in myclaims, iMaintain and Exchequer. Around ten admin staff review their own department’s sheets in a console with separate views for admin, accounts, payroll, IT and the trades themselves. Accounts give the final sign-off, and every Tuesday at 2pm a scheduled job posts the approved hours into Exchequer, ready for the payroll run. Everyone can see who submitted what, who approved it and when.

  • Node API on Linode
  • C# integration services
  • SQL Server
  • Exchequer COM toolkit
  • Active Directory roles

Gelder Group · Insurance claims

myclaims: the system that won the clients

When Gelders needed a claims management system, an outside development company quoted six figures. I built it in-house instead. It took a year, and it was the first system I built there.

It turned out to be more than a cost saving. With its own claims platform, Gelders could start working directly with major insurers rather than only through brokers.
